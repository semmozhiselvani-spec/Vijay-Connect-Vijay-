FAQ

Chennai cab booking — quick answers.

Everything you need to know before booking a Vijay Connect ride.

Which areas do you cover in Chennai?

We focus on Chennai and selected nearby service areas such as Tambaram, Porur, Guindy, Adyar, OMR, ECR, Avadi and surrounding locations.

Are the fares fixed?

The displayed prices are starting fares. Final pricing can vary based on pickup and drop point, waiting, tolls, parking, timing and trip requirements.

Can I choose Mini, Sedan or SUV?

Yes. Select the vehicle card on the route page to choose the car that suits your budget, comfort and space requirement.

How do I book a route?

Choose your destination, select a car and tap the booking button. Vijay Connect will take the request forward through the booking/contact flow.

Do you provide airport pickup and drop?

Yes, airport-related Chennai trips can be requested. Share your pickup, drop, date and time so the team can confirm the ride.

Can I request an outstation trip?

Yes. Use the main booking flow to request an outstation journey and share your return date if required.
